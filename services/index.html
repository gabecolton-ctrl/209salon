Color & Lighten

Every color service starts with a personal consultation so your shade, placement and upkeep fit your life.

Balayage / Foilayage

Soft, hand-painted or foiled brightness that grows out gracefully. Also with a cut, root color, all-over color, or combinations.

Full Foil

Maximum dimension from front to back. Also with a cut, root color, or both.

Partial Foil

Brightness around the face and top. Also with a cut, root color, or both.

Accent Foil

A few strategic pieces for a lift. Also with a cut, root color, or both.

Babylights

Ultra-fine highlights for a natural, sun-kissed blend. Also with a cut.

All Over Color

One rich, even shade. Also with a cut, or paired with accent, partial or full foils.

Base Color / Grey Coverage

Root-to-end coverage for greys or a refreshed base. Root color is also available with a cut.

Global Lightener

All-over lightening for platinum and bright blonde. Also with a cut.

Lightener Retouch

Keeps your blonde seamless at the root. Also with a cut.

Glaze / Gloss / Toner & Blow Dry

Shine, tone correction and a fresh finish between color visits.

Men's Color

Extensions

Certified extension artists and premium Remy hair. Every extension journey begins with a complimentary consultation.

Methods, care & consultation

Beaded Weft (Sew-In)

Seamless volume and length, installed on beaded rows.

Hand-Tied Wefts

Woven onto a fine foundation for a flat, natural finish.

Tape-In

Pre-taped wefts placed between thin sections of your hair. Low maintenance.

I-Tip

Small bundles attached with microbeads. Reusable.

Keratin Bonded K-Tip

Long-wearing individual bonds. New tips each install.

Extension Maintenance
