Extensions

Do I need a consultation first?
Yes, and it's complimentary. We look at your hair type, talk through the look you want, recommend a method, and give you a price estimate.
Which extension method is right for me?
It depends on your hair's density, texture and lifestyle. We offer beaded weft (sew-in), hand-tied, tape-in, I-tip and K-tip, with options for fine, medium and thick hair in a range of textures and colors.
How long do extensions last?
High-quality extensions can last 6–9 months or longer with proper care and regular maintenance.
How often do I need maintenance?
Every 4–6 weeks for hand-tied and I-tip, and every 6–10 weeks for tape-in.
Are extensions safe for my natural hair?
When they're installed and maintained by a trained professional, extensions are generally safe for your natural hair.
Can I color, curl and heat-style them?
Yes — extensions can be styled, colored, curled, straightened and blow-dried. Use a heat protectant and keep heat moderate.
Can extensions be reused?
Some methods can be removed and reused, like I-tips. K-tips cannot — they're replaced with each install.
How do I care for them at home?
Brush gently with a loop brush or wide-tooth comb, use sulfate- and alcohol-free products, use a heat protectant, and protect your hair while sleeping and swimming.
