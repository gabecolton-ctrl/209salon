Good to know

Salon policies

A few guidelines that help us give every guest our best.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Life happens. If you need to cancel or move your appointment, please let us know as early as you can so we can offer your time to another guest. You can reschedule online or by calling (972) 548-2815.

Late arrivals

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. If you're running late, give us a call — we'll do our best to complete your full service, but we may need to adjust or reschedule so we stay on time for the guests after you.

Deposits

Some longer services, such as extensions and larger color appointments, may require a deposit when you book. Any deposit is shown in online booking before you confirm.

Your satisfaction

We want you to love your hair. If something isn't right, please contact your studio within a few days of your visit and we'll make it right.

Guests & children

To keep the salon relaxing for everyone, we ask that only guests receiving services join us in the studio.

Full booking terms are shown when you book online.
