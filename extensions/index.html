Aftercare

Make them last.

With good care, high-quality extensions can last 6–9 months or longer.

  • Brush gently with a loop brush or wide-tooth comb
  • Use sulfate- and alcohol-free shampoo and conditioner
  • Always use a heat protectant and keep heat moderate
  • Braid or wrap your hair for sleep and swimming
  • Keep up with your maintenance appointments
