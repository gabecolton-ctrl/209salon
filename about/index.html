Our story

A family-owned salon with McKinney roots, built on education, customer service and great hair.

Since 2013

From downtown McKinney to five studios.

209 Salon began in downtown McKinney. In 2013, Rich Whitaker took ownership and made it a private family business, and today Rich and Emilie Whitaker lead 209 together.

Since then, 209 has grown into five studios across McKinney, Prosper, Plano, Frisco and Melissa — and became known along the way for its signature flower wall photos.

Every detail at 209 is built around two things: education and customer service. Our stylists keep learning, and we're proud of a team that brings together talent from across the United States and beyond.
